Our Story

Aapki Pasand means "your choice" – and that is exactly what we serve. Our shop in Darjeeling brings together the sweets of Bengal, the snacks of North India and the flavours of the hills.

Our specialities:

  • Bengali Sweets
    • Rasgulla
    • Sandesh
    • Mishti Doi
  • Hill Specials
    • Veg Momos
    • Sel Roti
    • Darjeeling Tea
