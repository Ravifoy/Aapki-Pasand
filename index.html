Welcome to Aapki Pasand

Aapki Pasand is a sweet shop in the heart of Darjeeling. From soft Rasgullas and creamy Mishti Doi to hot Samosas and steaming Momos, every item is prepared fresh in our own kitchen. For festivals, weddings and birthdays we also make custom gift boxes.
